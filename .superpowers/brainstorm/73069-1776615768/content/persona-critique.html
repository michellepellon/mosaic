Persona Critique: What a Serious Amateur Athlete Would Change

Thinking as a 40-year-old data-obsessed athlete who reads Attia and trains with purpose

"This is a health dashboard, not a training tool."

1. I think in questions, not metrics

The grid shows me 8 isolated numbers. But when I open this tool I'm asking:

  • "Can I go hard today?" → readiness (already have this)
  • "Am I recovering from last week?" → HRV + RHR + sleep as a unit, not 3 separate cards
  • "Is my aerobic base building?" → VO2 Max trend + Zone 2 volume together
  • "How was this training week?" → weekly review, not just today's snapshot

Fix: Organize by questions/stories, not by metric names. Group the cards into clusters that answer a question together.

2. Where's my training log?

"Exercise: 143 min/wk" tells me nothing. I want to see:

  • Mon: 45min run (Z2, avg HR 138)
  • Wed: 30min strength
  • Fri: 60min bike (Z2, avg HR 135)
  • Sat: 8min HIIT (Z4-5, avg HR 168)

Fix: A "This Week" training log is the most obvious missing piece. The data is in the workouts table already.

3. The expanded view shows one metric — but metrics interact

If I click "HRV" and see an HRV chart in isolation, I'm missing the point. The HRV drop might be fine (hard training week, expected) or concerning (sleep fell apart). I need HRV in context of sleep and training load — exactly what the correlation charts show.

Fix: When I expand a metric, show the related metrics alongside it. Don't just zoom into one number — zoom into the story around it.

4. Labs don't belong in the daily grid

My labs change every 6 months. Body fat changes monthly. Steps change hourly. These have completely different time scales. Putting "Glucose: 85 mg/dL" next to "Steps: 8,421" in the same grid makes no sense.

Fix: Labs get their own space — maybe a "Last Blood Draw" summary card that only appears when you have lab data, separate from the daily metrics.

5. I need a weekly review, not just a daily snapshot

The daily brief is my morning check. But I also sit down Sunday night and review the week: total training volume, sleep consistency, HRV trend across the week, whether I'm adapting or overreaching. This is a different mode — not "what should I do today" but "how did this week go?"

Fix: A toggle between "Today" and "This Week" view. The brief adapts: daily = readiness + prescription. Weekly = volume + recovery trend + adaptation signal.

Revised Structure

What the athlete actually wants to see
Brief — Score, prescription, alerts (today or this week toggle)
Recovery — HRV + RHR + Sleep as a unit. "Am I recovered?"
Training — This week's workout log + zone distribution + load trend. "What did I do?"
Fitness — VO2 Max + walking speed + body comp. "Am I getting fitter?"
Bloodwork — Last draw summary + trends for flagged tests. (Separate from daily metrics)
Console — ⌘K or always-visible SQL workspace
Each section is a collapsible card showing a 1-line summary when collapsed.
Click to expand with full charts + contextual metrics + editable SQL.
